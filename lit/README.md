# Literature provenance

`references.bib` is the manuscript bibliography. `literature-map.md` records the broader evidence search, the role of each source, exclusions, and the manuscript's core citation set. Titles, journal details, and DOIs were checked against publisher, journal, PubMed, institutional-repository, or Crossref records in August 2026.

The search covered four bodies of work needed to interpret the archival experiments:

- the original Asian Disease Problem and prospect theory;
- meta-analytic and taxonomic work on risky-choice framing;
- studies of option completeness, complements, order, probability, and wording variation;
- studies of individual differences and the composition of online panels.

The manuscript uses this literature to separate three claims: whether the framing contrast has the classic direction, whether its magnitude is constant across wordings, and whether education or political knowledge moderates it. The bibliography should be rechecked when a target journal is chosen and before submission, especially for online-first articles whose volume or page metadata may change.
